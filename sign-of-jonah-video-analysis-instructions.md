# Instructions for MiMo-V2.5: Sign of Jonah Video Analysis

## Video to Analyze
URL: https://youtu.be/oPJmV95giS4
Title: "Revelation 12:1-2 Sign — September 23, 2017" by Scottie Clarke (ERF Ministries)

## Task
Analyze this video visually and structurally. This is for a web developer who needs to recreate the celestial visualization approach using Three.js. Describe everything in precise, implementable detail.

## What to Extract

### 1. Constellation Rendering
- How are Virgo and Leo rendered? (Lines between stars? Glowing dots? Full artistic illustrations?)
- Are star labels shown? Font style, size, color, placement?
- Are constellation boundary lines drawn?
- How is the "crown of 12 stars" shown (9 Leo stars + 3 planets)?

### 2. Celestial Mechanics Animation
- How is Jupiter's retrograde motion through Virgo animated?
- How is the sun shown "clothing" the woman? (Corona effect? Actual star position?)
- How is the moon positioned at Virgo's feet?
- Any orbit trails, path lines, or motion markers?

### 3. Camera & Viewpoint
- Fixed geocentric view from Jerusalem?
- Orbiting/moving camera?
- Zoom transitions between macro (full sky) and micro (constellation detail)?
- Timeline slider or date counter?

### 4. Text & Callout Style
- Font families, sizes, colors
- How are scripture references displayed?
- How are dates shown?
- Any animated callout lines or pointers?
- Lower-third title style?

### 5. Color Palette & Lighting
- Background color (space black, deep blue, gradient?)
- Star colors (white, blue-white, gold?)
- Planet colors (Jupiter bands visible?)
- Accent colors for highlights
- Any glow, bloom, or haze effects

### 6. Timeline & Narrative Structure
- How is the 42-week gestation period visualized?
- How is the Sign of Jonah 40-day connection shown?
- Any countdown timer or progress bar?
- Transitions between topics

### 7. Specific Technical Observations
- Any 3D depth or is it 2D sky charts?
- Resolution of star background (real star map vs artistic)
- Any particle effects, shooting stars, or atmospheric effects
- Loading/transition animations

## Output Format
Write a structured, detailed description. Be specific about measurements, positions, and visual relationships — not just "it looks nice." The developer needs to rebuild this visual approach from scratch.
