The Rapture

ἁρπαγή σωματος

Harpazo — the catching away

"For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in Christ shall rise first: Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air."

The word rapture comes from the Latin rapio, translating the Greek harpazo — to seize, snatch away, or plunder. It is God plundering the earth by taking His greatest treasure out before judgment falls.

01 Chapter One

A Pattern Repeated

Before every major judgment, God removes His faithful. Not protecting them through it — removing them before it. The rapture is not a strange, isolated promise. It is the final instance of a pattern woven through all of Scripture.

01

Enoch Before the Flood

"Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him." The world drowned, but Enoch was taken first.

Genesis 5:24 (KJV)
02

Noah Before the Deluge

"Come thou and all thy house into the ark… Yet seven days, and I will cause it to rain." Noah was sealed in before the flood came.

Genesis 7:1,4 (KJV)
03

Lot Before Sodom

"The men put forth their hand, and pulled Lot into the house to them, and shut to the door." Sealed in for safety before judgment fell.

Genesis 19:10 (KJV)
04

Elijah Before Jehoram's Judgment

"There appeared a chariot of fire… and Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven." Taken just before judgment fell on Israel's kings.

2 Kings 2:11 (KJV)
05

Rahab Before Jericho's Fall

"By faith the harlot Rahab perished not with them that believed not." The scarlet thread marked her household for deliverance before the walls fell.

Hebrews 11:31 · Joshua 6:17 (KJV)
06

The Church Before the Tribulation

"For God hath not appointed us to wrath, but to obtain salvation by our Lord Jesus Christ." The pattern completes.

1 Thessalonians 5:9 (KJV)
As Steve Cioccolanti teaches: "Pre-trib, mid-trib, and post-trib — they're all there in the Bible. The difference between them is about three and a half years. The pattern is the message: every arrow is placed just before a judgment."

The pattern is undeniable: God removes before He judges. But when is the next removal? The answer is written into God's own calendar.

05 Chapter Five

The Case for Pre-Tribulation

The pre-trib view holds that the Church is caught up before the seven-year tribulation begins. Six pillars of evidence support this position — from Scripture, from the feasts, and from the consistent pattern of God's character.

✖

Not Appointed Unto Wrath

"For God hath not appointed us to wrath, but to obtain salvation by our Lord Jesus Christ." The tribulation is a time of wrath (Revelation 6:16-17). The Church is not appointed to that period.

1 Thessalonians 5:9 (KJV)
✖

Kept From the Hour of Trial

"I also will keep thee from the hour of temptation, which shall come upon all the world." The promise is removal from the hour, not protection through it.

Revelation 3:10 (KJV)
✖

The Historical Pattern

Enoch before the flood. Noah sealed in before the deluge. Lot before Sodom. Elijah before judgment. Rahab before Jericho. The biblical pattern is deliverance before judgment.

Genesis 5:24 · 7:1 · 19:10 · 2 Kings 2:11 (KJV)
✖

Revelation 6 is Pre-Trib

As Cioccolanti teaches: the six seals are pre-trib signs. At the 6th seal, the cry goes out "the great day of his wrath is come" — confirming wrath hadn't started yet. The 7th seal silence is the rapture.

Revelation 6:12-17, 8:1 (KJV)
✖

A Comforting Doctrine

Paul says: "Wherefore comfort one another with these words." A doctrine promising the Church will endure the tribulation is the opposite of comforting.

1 Thessalonians 4:18 (KJV)
✖

The Feast of Trumpets

The rapture aligns with the Feast of Trumpets — the one feast whose day no one knows. "No man knows the day or hour" points to Yom Teruah, which also begins the tribulation.

Matthew 24:36 · Leviticus 23:24 (KJV)
These views align with Steve Cioccolanti, Pastor Jack Hibbs, Dr. David Jeremiah, and the late Kim Clement — who repented of his earlier lack of rapture study and gravitated to the pre-trib view before his death, saying through Alan DiDio: "It's the only view that allows you to interpret the Word of God with the honor that it deserves."

The evidence for pre-trib is strong. But the Bible shows other raptures too — for different groups, at different times. The pre-trib view does not exclude them. It completes the picture.

08 Chapter Eight

What Must I Do To Be Saved?

You have seen the signs. The data is before you. The calendar converges. But knowing the season is not the same as being ready for it. The most important question is not when the rapture will happen — it is whether you are ready for it.

01

The Problem — Sin

"For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God."

Romans 3:23 (KJV)

Every person who has ever lived (except Jesus Christ) has sinned. Sin is not just "doing bad things" — it is rebellion against a holy God. We are all guilty.

02

The Penalty — Death

"For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord."

Romans 6:23 (KJV)

The payment our sin earns is death — physical death and eternal separation from God in the lake of fire (Revelation 20:15 KJV). There is nothing we can do to earn our way out of this penalty.

03

The Remedy — Christ

"But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us."

Romans 5:8 (KJV)

Jesus Christ — fully God, fully man — lived a sinless life and died on the cross as the perfect sacrifice for your sins. He paid the penalty you could not pay. He rose from the dead, defeating death forever.

04

The Response — Repentance & Faith

"That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved."

Romans 10:9 (KJV)

Salvation is not about being good enough, going to church, or saying religious words. It is about placing your full trust in Jesus Christ alone — believing He died for your sins and rose again, and confessing Him as your Lord.

"For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast." — Ephesians 2:8-9 (KJV)

If You Are Left Behind

If you read these words after the rapture has taken place, do not lose hope. Salvation is still available during the tribulation — but it will cost you everything. Millions will reject the mark of the beast and give their lives for their faith (Revelation 20:4 KJV). The two witnesses will preach in Jerusalem (Revelation 11:3 KJV). The 144,000 Jewish evangelists will proclaim the gospel to every nation (Revelation 7:2-8 KJV).

"And I said unto him, Sir, thou knowest. And he said to me, These are they which came out of great tribulation, and have washed their robes, and made them white in the blood of the Lamb."

Revelation 7:14 (KJV)

Do not take the mark of the beast. Do not worship the image. Endure to the end. Your salvation is worth more than your life.

A Prayer to Receive Christ

If you believe these things are true and want to receive Jesus Christ as your Lord and Savior, pray this from your heart:

Lord Jesus, I confess that I am a sinner. I believe that You died for my sins and rose again from the dead. I repent of my sin and turn to You. I ask You to forgive me and cleanse me. I receive You as my Lord and Savior. Write my name in the Lamb's Book of Life. Help me to live for You and to be ready when You return. In Jesus' name, Amen.

"He that believeth on the Son hath everlasting life: and he that believeth not the Son shall not see life; but the wrath of God abideth on him."

John 3:36 (KJV)

Do not delay. "Behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation" (2 Corinthians 6:2 KJV). The rapture could happen at any moment. Be ready.

You have heard the gospel. You have seen the signs. Now watch, and be ready.

"And what I say unto you I say unto all, Watch."

Mark 13:37 (KJV)

The data is here. The pattern is visible. The calendar converges. The question is not whether the signs are here — but what you will do with them. Be ready. Be watching. Be found faithful.
