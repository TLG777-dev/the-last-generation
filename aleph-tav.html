The 1806/1811 Precedent A Warning Repeated and Escalated

This was not the first time an X formed over this land. In 1806, a total solar eclipse predicted by the Shawnee prophet Tenskwatawa crossed the United States from Baja California to Massachusetts. In 1811, a second eclipse completed a paleo-Hebrew Tav (X) over the New Madrid fault zone. Three months later, the 1811-1812 New Madrid earthquakes struck — the largest seismic event in American history.

The 2017-2024 pattern repeats the X but adds a third line (the 2023 annular), elevating it from a mere warning cross (Tav) to a completed divine signature (Aleph-Tav). What was a warning then has become a signed declaration. Watchmen including Brandon Biggs have likewise pointed to the X over Little Egypt and the New Madrid fault as a sign of impending seismic judgment, echoing the 1811-1812 precedent.

"Oth" — Sign and Letter

The Hebrew word אות (oth) appears in Genesis 1:14 KJV as "signs" (‏אותות‎, othoth). The exact same word means "letters" of the alphabet. The firmament is God's parchment. The sun and moon are His pen. Eclipses are His alphabet.

The Aleph-Tav (את) is the untranslated direct object marker embedded in the very first verse of Scripture:

“In the beginning, God את created the heavens and the earth.”

Jesus declares in Revelation 1:8 KJV: "I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the ending." In Hebrew, the conceptual equivalent is Aleph and Tav — the first and last letters of the Hebrew alphabet, the same letters that bracket Genesis 1:1 KJV. The apostle John would have understood Jesus to be saying: "I am the Aleph and the Tav — the One who was, who is, and who is to come." The same signature that opens Scripture has been written across this nation by the moon's shadow.

In the ancient paleo-Hebrew script, the Aleph (𐤀) was drawn as the head of an ox — strength and authority. The Tav (𐤕) was drawn as an X or cross — the sign of the covenant, the mark of God. The cross formed by the 2017 and 2024 eclipse paths is not merely an abstract letter: it is the paleo-Hebrew Tav, the ancient sign of the covenant, the very mark that Ezekiel 9 describes the angel placing on the foreheads of those who grieved over the nation's sins (Ezekiel 9:4-6 KJV). What the moon has drawn over America is the cross of Christ (John 6:35 KJV; Acts 4:12 KJV), at a scale visible from space.
