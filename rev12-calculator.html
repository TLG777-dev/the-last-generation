Revelation 12:1–2 KJV

"And there appeared a great wonder in heaven; a woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars: And she being with child cried, travailing in birth, and pained to be delivered."

What Does This Mean?

John describes a celestial picture: the constellation Virgo (the woman) standing with the Sun near her torso ("clothed with the sun"), the Moon at her feet, and Jupiter (the King Planet, representing the Messiah) in her womb. Above her head, three planets in Leo join its nine main stars — forming a crown of twelve.

This precise alignment — Sun in Virgo, Moon at her feet, Jupiter in Virgo, and a crown in Leo — is astronomically rare. It requires multiple planets and the Moon to be in exact positions simultaneously. The question is: when in history has this happened?

September 23, 2017

On this date, the sky over Jerusalem displayed a remarkable configuration matching John's description. The Sun was in Virgo, the Moon was at Virgo's feet, Jupiter had been "in the womb" of Virgo for approximately 9 months, and Venus, Mars, and Mercury formed a crown in Leo.

This was the first time in over 1,000 years that all four elements of the sign appeared together. It was visible from Jerusalem — the city John was standing in when he received the vision.
