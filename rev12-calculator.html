01

The Prophecy

In Revelation 12:1–2, the Apostle John describes a celestial picture visible from Jerusalem: the constellation Virgo (the woman) standing with the Sun across her body ("clothed with the sun"), the Moon beneath her feet, and Jupiter — the King Planet — in her womb. Above her, planets in Leo join its stars to form a crown of twelve.

This is not vague symbolism. It is a precise astronomical configuration that either happens in the sky — or it doesn't. The question: how often?

02

The Date

On September 23, 2017, the sky over Jerusalem displayed exactly what John described. The Sun was in Virgo. The Moon was at Virgo's feet. Jupiter — which had been "in the womb" of Virgo for approximately 9 months — was in position. Venus, Mars, and Mercury formed a crown in Leo.

It was the first time in over 1,000 years that all four elements appeared together, visible from the exact city where John received the vision.
